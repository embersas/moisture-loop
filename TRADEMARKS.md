# SoilSync name and branding policy

This policy is separate from the software licence. It describes use of the
SoilSync project identity; it does not add terms to the GNU General Public
License.

## Software and branding are separate

The SoilSync source code is licensed under GPL-3.0-only. The GPL grants rights
to use, study, modify, and distribute that software under its terms. It does
not, merely by licensing the code, grant rights to use the SoilSync name, logo,
or official project branding as the identity of another distribution.

The SoilSync name, SoilSync logo, and official SoilSync project branding
identify the official SoilSync project maintained at
<https://github.com/embersas/soilsync>. This policy makes no claim that any
mark is registered.

## Forks and derivative distributions

Forks and derivative distributions may exercise all rights granted by
GPL-3.0-only. They should use names, logos, and presentation sufficiently
distinct to avoid suggesting that they are the official SoilSync project.
They must not falsely describe a fork as an official SoilSync release or imply
sponsorship, endorsement, or affiliation that does not exist.

Factual references such as "based on SoilSync" or "compatible with SoilSync"
are not intended to be prohibited where legally permitted, provided they do
not create a false impression of official status or endorsement.

## Scope

This policy concerns project identity and branding only. It does not restrict
or modify any right granted to the software source code by GPL-3.0-only.
